Setup

From bag to first rep in under a minute

No drilling, no brackets, nothing left behind on the wall. XBound Elite sets up on whatever's in front of you.

01

Loop or lean

Stretch the mesh around your hoop's rim, or unfold the frame and lean it flat against any wall or fence.

02

Clip in the Return Core

Snap the weighted core into your ball's valve pocket. One core, works across your basketball, soccer ball, or footy.

03

Shoot, kick, throw

The angled mesh catches the ball and redirects it downward, so it rolls back along the ground instead of bouncing away.

04

Fold it flat

Collapses into its carry bag in seconds. Move it to the next hoop, wall, or backyard fence and go again.
